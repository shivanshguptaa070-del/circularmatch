Slide 1 of 7 — Problem Statement

India's ₹80,000 Cr industrial waste market runs on WhatsApp, phone calls & handwritten receipts.

The real problem is not discovery. It is qualification.

🚫

30–60% Rejected at the Gate

Industrial buyers reject 30–60% of material upon arrival — wrong grade, mismatched technical specs, or undisclosed contamination.

📜

SEBI Mandate, Zero Digital Documentation

Top 1,000 listed companies must prove waste recycling in annual BRSR audits, yet rely on unverified manual chits.

⚖️

Brokers Create Regulatory Liability

Brokers move material fast but produce no compliant paper trail — creating direct regulatory liability under SEBI.

Bottom line: Factories have material. Buyers have requirements. Nobody can prove the material actually meets them.

Slide 3 of 7 — Target Users

Two sides. One regulatory & financial imperative.

Solving compliance failure for sellers and gate rejection financial loss for buyers.

🏭 SUPPLIER SIDE EHS / Operations Manager

Profile: BSE/NSE-listed factory in Noida generating recurring industrial scraps (PET, cotton, corrugated) with mandatory SEBI BRSR Core filing.

⚠️ Pain Point: BRSR auditors reject broker receipts. They need verifiable digital chain-of-custody for statutory audits — currently zero digital records exist.
🔄 BUYER SIDE Procurement / Sourcing Manager

Profile: Sourcing manager at registered recyclers or brand owners building EPR compliance supply chains needing pre-qualified lots.

📉 Pain Point: 30–60% gate rejection rate on unverified lots. Every rejected truckload incurs 100% freight cost with zero usable material.
Why these two: The supplier has a regulatory mandate making documentation non-optional. The buyer has a financial loss making pre-qualification worth paying for.

"2 tonnes cotton cutting waste from Noida, Grade A" → extracted into schema instantly
Feature 2 — Match Explanation ("Why this match?"):
Deterministic 6-D score passed to Gemini → 2–3 sentence breakdown of strengths and risks. AI explains the score — does not determine it.
🛡️ Reliability: Gemini fails → rule-based extractor takes over automatically.
